# @webship-js/leantime-steps

Cucumber step definitions for Leantime, the open-source project management software, a pack for
[webship-js](https://github.com/webship/webship-js) (Playwright and Cucumber): logging in, a project and who may open
it, its tasks and one task, its status, comments and assignees, a new task, and its milestones.

The steps, their documentation and CI come with the first release, `2.0.0`.
